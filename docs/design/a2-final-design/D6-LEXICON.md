## 2. D6-FA-r01 disambiguation

### 2.1 Authority Store no longer means “body SQLite”

The controlled AuthorityStore/authorityStore names remain because historical D6/D3/D7 text and saved evidence use them. D6-FA-r01 narrows the meaning to Core’s logical view over current author/portable-control truth:

- current Document/Resource bytes: ordinary files;
- identity/parent/order/lifecycle/shared policy/trust: Portable Workspace Metadata;
- operation decision/unknown/approval/Money: Durable Control Store;
- index/cache: Derived Index Store.

AuthorityStore must therefore never be read as “a SQLite table containing the current body”, and DurableControlStore never owns current portable parent/order.

### 2.2 Storage Domain and Commit Domain

StorageDomain remains the historical physical/recovery coordination concept. CommitDomain is the new FA-r01 idempotency/version namespace. A file-backed Workspace may coordinate ordinary files, portable metadata and off-workspace durable control as one managed operation while the operation key still explicitly contains CommitDomain. Neither concept is Workspace identity.

ReplicaEpoch denotes an ordinary replica writer generation. ExecutionResponsibility denotes continuous Automation/approval/Money/external-effect responsibility. Neither implies the other, and loss of execution responsibility does not freeze unrelated ordinary content.

### 2.3 Source Version

The SourceVersion/1 controlled names remain for legacy decoders. SourceVersion/2 is the complete production-version union. Its managed arm stores full EntityRef, production CommitDomain, production observationEpoch, managed revision, and ChangeId. Its external arm stores full EntityRef, production CommitDomain, production observationEpoch, and externalSequence and has no managed revision or ChangeId. A SourceVersion/2 production domain may differ from the current operation/observer domain, and externalSequence never substitutes for a D3/D4/D5 managed sourceRevision.

For production domain D and entity E, H(D,E) is the greatest revision of E in D's continuous sealed managed production history. H=0 is a complete empty history only when domain birth/registration, P continuity, and verified portable sealed history prove that D has never sealed a managed version for E; missing, corrupt, gapped, or otherwise unknown history is never empty. A new managed after uses checked H+1, MAX never wraps, and production observationEpoch changes do not reset H inside a production domain. When an existing source is first written by another production domain, the new domain uses its own H+1 and later cross-domain returns continue each domain's own H; equal revision numbers in different production domains are not equal versions. A true raw no-op preserves the original SourceVersion/2. Pure placement/lifecycle/control with unchanged source does not advance H. Source deletion has after=absent, creates no deleted SourceVersion, and does not advance H. Equal-byte external admission is still explicit managed admission and creates the managed after from the current production domain's H+1.

Current replica/Server qualification is separately bound by the complete SourceObservation/1: observerDomain, EntityRef, the production SourceVersion/2, current-observer observationEpoch, FileObjectBinding, and evidence pins. SourceVersion.observationEpoch and SourceObservation.observationEpoch serve different roles even when numerically equal. Watcher/journal gaps, external/object replacement, placeholder state changes, or discontinuous rematerialization invalidate the old current observation; equal production version, revision, digest, or final text never revives it. SourceVersionRef/1 sourceToken selects this complete protected Observation and is not a bare revision, digest, I cache, or production SourceVersion.

Only a plan that will actually produce a new managed after stores internal `SourceRevisionPlan/1` in P and freezes one proposed `RevisionTokenBinding/2` before C/Q position materialization. The binding contains only token+`RevisionTokenSource/2` and is a stable production address, not current observer qualification. The winning planning CAS freezes that exact binding with the version basis and pins. At the sole P seal, every actual managed after gets one closed `RevisionTokenSealAssociation/1` inside one signed `RevisionTokenSealArtifact/1`; the exact artifact bytes are authenticated with the historical portable-trust key selected by `RevisionTokenSealVerificationKey/1`, pinned as portable metadata, and referenced by `RevisionTokenSealOutboxItem/1`. The signature message is domain-separated D3-CJ/3 canonical bytes, so equal SourceStamp/SourceVersion/digest or sender trust cannot authenticate another token. Losers, aborted plans and equal-stamp/H+1 reuse never borrow another seal. Publication/retry/I rebuild reuse the original artifact bytes and never re-sign. A new observer on a current record verifies that artifact and CP4/ChangeRecord1 independently, cross-checks the exact production version, and then proves a fresh current `SourceObservation/1`; genuine historical CP3 remains version-qualified; this may qualify a new read but never revives an old selector, ActionEvidence, Draft/map, PAB or PreparedIntent. Legacy d6d/d6r/d6a profiles, D3 Locator opaque revision-token lexical ownership, and D4/D5 inner sourceRevision/selector wire remain under their original owners and shapes.
### 2.4 Reliable Save and Portable Publication

ReliableSaveState is independent of ContentGuarantee, SemanticState, and WriteProtection. not_saved means no successful file save. reliable comes only from strict installation plus P seal. durable_observed_only comes only from a fully qualified human ordinary-file weak-protection save plus seal. not_applicable is for paths with no applicable file-save effect. Successful prepare, PreparedIntent/input retention, Draft persistence, worker/HTTP success, InstallationNotice, or sync upload is not Saved, and portable publication has its own state.

observed_only is limited to a trusted interactive_source_save human who explicitly selects and freezes it before planning starts, targeting exactly one existing live Document as ordinary+replica_local whole-source save, with complete source read/replace, author-source write set empty or limited to that Document, no applicable body/Field/node-control deny, no identity/parent/order/lifecycle/shared-policy/Registry/Calendar-scope/other-entity mutation, and Draft Base equal to the selected current SourceObservation. noninteractive, D3 identity/structure/lifecycle, D5 structured operations, bulk/collection, D7 strong Action, Automation, server checkpoint, Approval, and Money remain strict. Strict failure, known conflict, revocation, durability failure, or a failed strong obligation after planning starts never falls back to observed_only.

For a qualified observed_only plan, the actually read before B and user input N remain durably retained. The sole relaxation is that an external C never observed after the final trusted check and before N is installed may be overwritten by N and may have no recoverable copy. A later C may replace current file again, but the durable B/N obligation remains. Observed competition, stale Base, watcher gap, third_state, or revocation is outside the relaxation. Unknown installation remains recovery_unknown and equal hash never guesses success.

A planned record resumes only the same frozen plan: original InputDescriptor, applicable SourceRevisionPlan/SourceStamp, before/after pins, OperationId, budget/attempt state, WriteProtection, InstallationNotice, and installation state. It never prepares a second decision and has no preallocated ChangeId. A saved/committed record, after original request/fingerprint/continuity lookup, replays original receipt/error/effects bytes or resumes its original-version publication/outbox under current delivery authorization for the original actual effect/mode or result-disclosure scope. It does not require the old source/Frontier/business proof to equal current state, does not rewrite a later current source, does not reallocate versions, and never charges again. Revocation may hide delivery without rewriting history. Unknown and legacy pin/recovery obligations are not erased by new wire, I rebuild, or reauthorization.

A new FA portable decision publishes ContentCompletionProof/4 only after seal. publication pending resumes only the same sealed decision's proof/outbox. Neither reliable nor durable_observed_only automatically means portable published.
### 2.5 Semantic State

Both complete_semantics and semantic_pending require D2 validity. pending lists closed, explicitly unproved obligations; it is not a free “check later” string.

external_invalid is not SemanticState because it has no successful Core author decision. It is a branch of CurrentSourceState.

semantic_pending is never consumed as complete by complete D7 Query/Action, purge, relation/unique/Calendar mutation, or automatic writes. Any local consumer must be explicitly frozen by its D4/D5/D7/D8 owner.

## 3. Technical ownership

- D6 owns commit-domain, observation, and production-version control types: CommitDomain, DecisionKey/2, ReplicaEpoch, ChangeId, Frontier/2, SourceVersion/2, SourceObservation/1, and SourceVersionRef/1. SourceStamp/1, internal SourceRevisionPlan/1, RevisionTokenBinding/2, and d6_source_revision/2 also have their technical shapes frozen by D6 Control but gain no new public conceptId.
- D6 owns semantic/dependency proof carriers: SemanticState, ContentGuarantee, ObservationScope/2, DependencyProof/3, DependencyKey/3, and WriteProtection. D6 owns the fifteen closed DependencyKey/3 carrier kinds, including document_format, plus ordering, stamps, persistence, and commit/recovery validation; concrete StructureRange, D4 relation/calendar, D7 query_scan and other enumeration algorithms remain with their real owners and are never replaced by free JSON.
- D6 owns current installation, completion, conflict, and execution-continuity carriers: InstallationNotice/3, ContentCompletionProof/4, ChangeRecord/1, ConflictRecord/2, ReliableSaveState, and ExecutionResponsibilityRecord/3. Genuine predecessor versions remain historical dispatch, not parallel generic current. Historical ContentCompletionProof/1,/2, ConflictRecord/1, Frontier/1, and InstallationNotice/1 retain their original decoders/bytes/pins and are never silently reinterpreted as current versions.
- NodeRef/ResourceRef/AnnotationRef, OperationId, AuthorityInstanceId, D3 lifecycle receipt, and D3 identity/placement/lifecycle producers remain D3-owned. A generic D6 commit, SourceRevisionPlan, or companion never expands D3 WriteScope. The current A2 D3 candidate uses wire13/`d3_identity_operation/13` and its bounded D3 findings are independently CLOSED; genuine wire9–12 records remain historical exact dispatch. This does not independently accept D6 or global A2.
- FieldId, RegistryBinding, and relation/Calendar typed semantics remain D4-owned. Concrete relation_incidence and calendar_scope enumeration remains D4-owned; D6 only carries the closed dependency key/stamp/pins and commit boundary.
- QuerySpec, ActionSpec, PreparedActionBinding, EffectManifest/EffectBytes, query_scan, and complete Result/Action selector, authorization-generation, positive/negative range, and reset rules remain D7-owned. Query/Value-CEL/View/Narrow Field/Definition Transfer/Preview-Effects/Execution-Action/Prepared/Scenarios/Lexicon-Registry/Impact still require their later complete consumer afterimages.
- Draft/Edit Map/IME/Undo/Source-Live-Read remain D8-owned. Import conversion inputs and ExportPlan/Publication remain D9-owned; D6 owns only durable control, pins, and budget boundaries.
- ApprovalUse, sourceOccurrenceKey, ToolValue, Money lineage, Agent/Automation/connector external effects remain D10/respective Money-owner responsibilities. D6 stores only the durable continuity container and no-double-consume/no-replay boundary; execution_resource or CommitDomain never substitutes for D10 Run/Lease/Automation/Money/unknown contracts.
## 4. Policy/3 controlled capabilities

Policy/3 retains every Policy/2 capability and adds:

| capability | meaning | does not imply |
|---|---|---|
| replica_register | explicitly register a new ReplicaEpoch | source read/write or execution takeover |
| replica_retire | retire an ordinary replica writer epoch | deletion of history or Money refund |
| conflict_read | read an authorized ConflictRecord | conflict source bytes |
| conflict_resolve | enter owner-specific resolution prepare | source/policy/D3 write |
| structure_state | observe portable parent/order and structural scope | source/Field/write |
| portable_frontier_state | observe complete Frontier/2 | decision/source/write |
| execution_custody_admin | manage execution-responsibility continuity/takeover | new approvals, larger Money, author write |
| d10_control_self | finite self-management of own D10 Workspace controls | content/resource/deployment authority |

In Policy/3, commit_sequence_state observes one named CommitDomain’s domainCommitSequence. The historical Policy/2 workspace-wide meaning remains only on its legacy path and is never projected onto the offline-replica model.

## 5. Collisions, aliases and migration

1. No local-workspace-id, device-workspace-id or sync-id becomes content identity.
2. ReplicaEpoch, AuthorityInstanceId and executionDomainId are distinct UUID domains.
3. ChangeId is never shortened to revision, commit id, operation id or sync token.
4. Frontier is never called global latest/version; it is a multi-domain causal frontier.
5. ConflictId is a stable address of a complete ConflictKey, not independent durable content identity.
6. DurableControlStore is not “the index”; DerivedIndexStore is not “database authority”.
7. Product text distinguishes Draft/input retained, strict reliable author save, observed_only ordinary-file save, and portable published.
