## 3. Identity and address domains

EntityRef, durable D3 Locator, LogicalOccurrenceKey, ResultRowHandle, internal provenance, and ActionEvidence are separate domains and never coerce into one another. ## 5. Query DAG, canonicalization, and execution order

A Query is one strict author DAG with local author node IDs. Canonical form is independent of author ID spelling: dependencies are validated first, then a deterministic topological order is chosen by the retained canonicalOrdinal rules, expression AST member ordering, unsigned UTF-8 member ordering, numeric list/index ordering, sharing identity, and the separate call namespace. Every semantically reachable branch keeps strict decode, type, feature, authorization, execution, and terminal checks. total and may_error are semantic properties, not optimizer hints. Budget exhaustion and cancellation are explicit outcomes. Optimizations may fuse or reorder only when bag multiplicity, output order, negative dependencies, authorization, errors including late errors, cancellation, and charged work remain observationally equal. Runtime budget is never rewritten into take, and an optimizer cannot skip a branch merely because earlier data appears sufficient.
